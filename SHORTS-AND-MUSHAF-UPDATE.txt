AMANORA SHORTS + MUSHAF UPDATE

QURAN READER
- The Quran tab now opens in a 15-line Madani Mushaf page view.
- All 604 page image URLs follow the repository's numbered image convention.
- Page navigation, Surah picker, recitation, saved pages, translations and text-study fallback remain available.
- Zoom controls are included.
- Page artwork source: https://github.com/akram-seid/quran-hd-images
- The repository describes the artwork as 15-line Madani Mushaf images generated using the official Quran.com image generator. Review the source repository's reuse terms before commercial distribution.
- Page artwork is loaded remotely, so an internet connection is required. If an image cannot load, the reader falls back to Quran text.

ISLAMIC SHORTS
- Shorts is now a primary bottom navigation destination.
- The feed retains the built-in daily narrated story cards, swipe-up paging, likes, share, speech narration and saved preferences.
- Users can add their own authorised YouTube or direct MP4 video links.
- The built-in story cards are animated text/emoji story experiences, not pre-recorded cartoon video files. Do not advertise them as professionally produced video episodes.
- Only upload or link videos you own or have permission to use. The app does not bundle Abdul Bari or other third-party copyrighted cartoons.
