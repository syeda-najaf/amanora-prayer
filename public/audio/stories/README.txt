AMANORA HUMAN AUDIO STORY RECORDINGS

Place human-recorded MP3 files in this folder. No AI-generated voices or YouTube audio.

For each Prophet story, add:
  <story-id>-en.mp3   English narration
  <story-id>-ur.mp3   Urdu narration

Example:
  adam-en.mp3
  adam-ur.mp3
  nuh-en.mp3
  nuh-ur.mp3

The story player in Prophet Stories selects the matching file automatically.
The story IDs are the IDs in src/components/ProphetStories.tsx.
Use clear narration, obtain the narrator's permission, and verify religious content and pronunciation before publishing.
No actual human recordings are bundled yet. The player will show the browser's audio controls but needs these MP3 files to play.
