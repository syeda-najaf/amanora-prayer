Amanora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Amanora is an Islamic companion app: prayer times, Quran reader, Hajj and Umrah guide, duas, learning and reflection tools. This page explains what data the app uses.

Data kept on your device

Your bookmarks, prayer log, Quran reading progress, journal, settings, Khatm plan and trip notes are stored only on your device (browser or app storage). Amanora does not upload them. You can delete them any time from Profile → Delete my saved data.

Location

If you allow it, your approximate location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Coordinates are sent to the prayer-time service (Aladhan) to return times and are not stored by Amanora on a server. You can use the app without location by choosing a city manually.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Amanora schedules local notifications on your device for prayer times, including an Azan sound. No notification data is sent to a server.

Accounts, subscriptions and shared content

You create an account to open Amanora. We store your name, email address, phone number, optional city and Plus subscription status with Supabase to run your account and activate your membership after payment. You can edit these details or permanently delete your account from Profile. Payments are made through UPI or the payment provider shown at checkout; Amanora does not see or store your card or bank details.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests data from: Aladhan (prayer times), alQuran Cloud and Quran.com (Quran text, translations and page layout), Islamic Network (recitation audio), and Supabase (accounts and shared content). These services receive standard request data such as your IP address.

Children

Amanora does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

No ads, no sale of data

Amanora shows no advertising and does not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can deny location and notification permissions, clear app data, or ask us to delete your account data by contacting us.
