# Amanora Original Learning Library

## Editorial promise
Amanora uses no third-party video embeds, creator clips, copied lectures or synthetic narration. The Read & Reflect area contains original educational explanations written for Amanora in English and Hindi. They are not quotations, formal tafsir, hadith grading or fatwas.

## Current original material
- 12 bilingual learning essays on mercy, Salah, gratitude, patience, parents, honesty, charity, learning, repentance, speech, balance and community.
- Each essay provides Quran references and one practical reflection action.
- Users can search, change language, save readings locally, and share a lesson.
- Existing bilingual Prophet stories, duas, quiz and hadith sections remain text-first.

## Source and research policy
- Quran references are identifiers for the primary source, not a substitute for reading the full verse and context.
- Quran Foundation documentation describes APIs for Quran text, translations, tafsir and related resources. Any future API integration must use credentials on a backend and respect translation integrity and attribution requirements: https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/api-reference/ and https://quran.com/developers
- Sunnah.com explains that its API requires an API key and that its data is manually checked in stages. Hadith references and grading should be checked against the collection and edition before publishing: https://sunnah.com/developers and https://sunnah.com/about
- Amanora must not invent Arabic text, hadith wording, narrator names, grades, scholarly consensus or legal rulings.
- Fiqh differences should be identified respectfully. Personal reflection must never be presented as a binding religious ruling.
- New material intended as tafsir, fatwa, or detailed Seerah should receive review by a qualified scholar before public release.

## Technical notes
- Saved reading IDs use the existing local-storage helper. They remain on the current browser/device and are not synced to an account.
- No iframe, external video feed or video player remains in the application source.
- Quran recitation remains separate from the original written learning library. Audio sources and licensing should be clearly attributed before commercial or offline distribution.
