# Amanora Bayan Studio update

Date: 2 November 2026

## Added to the existing app

- New Bayan Library screen, reachable from Explore and Amanora Plus.
- English full-lecture catalogue with speaker, duration, topic and date metadata.
- Curated links to full Mufti Menk audio pages hosted by Muslim Central. Mufti Menk's official website lists Muslim Central among his audio lecture destinations.
- Search and topic filters.
- Device-local saved favourites, recently opened items and listening queue.
- Urdu shelf placeholder with an explicit rights/editorial note; no Urdu audio is fabricated or mislabeled.
- Personal audio import for files the user owns or has permission to use. Selected files play locally in the current session; files are not uploaded.
- Local-file playback speed and sleep timer controls.
- Bayan Listening Studio added to Amanora Plus as a preview of the premium direction.

## Audio rights and limitations

Amanora does not copy or rehost third-party recordings. Full Mufti Menk lectures open on the publisher's page. The publisher controls the player, stream, download availability and terms. The in-app HTML audio controls currently work only with user-selected local audio files.

Do not scrape or hotlink protected MP3 files. Before adding an in-app remote stream or offline download, obtain written permission or a licence from the rights holder/publisher and store the approved media in an Amanora-controlled audio service.

The Urdu audio shelf is intentionally not populated with unverified recordings. Add Urdu Bayans only after verifying the speaker, source, language, full-length status and reuse permission.

## Premium product direction

Existing listed pricing remains ₹99/month and ₹799/year. The current UI still routes through the existing UPI checkout; this update does not implement payment verification or automatic entitlement activation.

Premium features worth building before charging for them:
1. Licensed full-length Bayan collections in English and Urdu, organised by scholar, topic and series.
2. Background audio playback with lock-screen controls and reliable resume position in a native app or supported media session.
3. Offline downloads with user-controlled storage, Wi-Fi-only downloads and automatic cleanup.
4. Smart playlists, bookmarks with timestamps, private notes and cross-device sync.
5. Structured 7/30-day learning journeys with reviewed lesson notes and knowledge checks.
6. Family learning circle with separate profiles, shared goals and parental controls.
7. Quran hifz revision planner, verse repeat and progress insights. Do not advertise AI mistake detection until a tested recitation service exists.
8. Ad-free experience and privacy-first analytics. Core Quran reading, Salah times and essential worship tools should remain accessible for free.

Only list offline, background, synced, AI or family-cloud features as included after they are implemented and tested. Religious lessons should be reviewed by qualified scholars; course completion must not be presented as scholarly certification or a fatwa.
