AMANORA DAILY AUTO-PILOT UPDATE

What changed
- Added a Daily Auto-Pilot screen accessible from the Home screen's daily reflection card.
- Selects a daily reflection, Hadith, and story using the local calendar date.
- The daily selection refreshes while the app remains open and automatically changes with the date.
- Includes browser text-to-speech playback for the selected English story.
- Provides shortcuts to the existing Quiz, Reels, Bayan, Listen, Stories, and Hadith sections.

Important scope and honesty
- This is a local, date-based daily rotation using content already bundled in the app. It does not create new religious content or upload/publish new items to all users.
- Hadith content is drawn from the existing AUTHENTIC_HADITHS collection and displayed with its existing reference. Please have qualified reviewers validate the app's existing religious content before public release.
- Browser speech synthesis depends on installed device voices and browser support. It is not a professionally recorded recitation.
- A real cloud auto-publishing system still needs a deployed backend, database, owner authentication, storage, a scheduled job, moderation workflow, and licensed media. Those cannot be made operational by frontend code alone.
- Real AI presenter video generation may require a paid provider or self-hosted compute. No paid service has been enabled.
- Existing local browser uploads are not automatically shared with other users.
